## 🌐 Live Website

🔗 **Website:** https://srkuexports.com/

Sri Kumaran Enterprises is an Indian export and distribution business focused on premium agricultural and food products for domestic and international markets.

The website is designed as a professional **B2B export platform**, allowing international buyers, importers, wholesalers, distributors, retailers, and food-service partners to explore products and submit export enquiries.

---

# 📌 About the Project

The **Sri Kumaran Enterprises website** is a modern corporate export website created to establish a strong digital presence for an Indian agricultural exporter.

The platform presents:

- Company information
- Export product catalogue
- Rice varieties
- Coconut products
- Fresh fruits
- Fresh vegetables
- Processed products
- Certifications
- Export capabilities
- Quality assurance
- Business advantages
- Vision and mission
- Export enquiry form
- Direct contact information

The website focuses on creating **trust, transparency and easy communication** between the company and global buyers.
